# GOST

A board for a big aim, and every step under it.

You start with the thing you want. Under that sit the big pieces. Under those sits the work. Under that sits the next small thing someone can do today. GOST keeps the whole chain on one board you share.

## The trip we keep talking about

Say the goal is **Take the trip we keep talking about.**

Open it and you plan the big pieces. Those are initiatives. Maybe **Pick where** and **Get there**.

Open **Get there** and you break it into epics. **Flights** and **The dog**.

Open **Flights** and you see tasks. **Find dates that work** and **Book the tickets**.

Open **Book the tickets** and the sub-tasks are the tiny steps. **Check the passport dates.** **Compare two airlines.** A sub-task is the end of the line. It holds a note and comments.

| You call it | It means | Open it and you see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goal | The aim | Initiatives |
| Initiative | A big piece of that aim | Epics |
| Epic | A chunk of work | Tasks |
| Task | Something a person can take | Sub-tasks |
| Sub-task | The next small step | A short summary. Edit holds the note and comments. |

Home is your goals. Open any card to go one level down. That page is a summary and the next level. Edit is a separate page for the note, the date, who is doing it, and comments.

## Where a card sits

Every board uses the same five columns, from left to right.

1. **Backlog.** We know about it. We are not doing it yet.
2. **Ready.** It can start.
3. **In progress.** Someone is on it.
4. **Blocked.** It stopped. It can start again.
5. **Done.** Finished.

Drag a card to another column, or change the status on the card. A goal can be In progress while the tasks under it are still in Backlog. Each card has its own place.

## See everything at once

The All view lays the whole plan on one screen. Each bigger item holds the smaller ones inside it. Urgent things sit higher. Open a title to land on that card's own board, which is where you add things and put them in order.

## What you write on a card

- A title
- How urgent it is, from 0 to 4. 0 is urgent. 4 is the lowest. A new card starts at 2, which means normal.
- A note
- A due date. If that day has passed and the card is not Done, it shows as late. A blocked card can be late too.
- One person who is doing it
- Colored labels, such as "money" or "outside"
- Comments, with the person's name and the time

## Who it is for

A few people share one board. Each person has an email and a password. The first visit creates the owner. The owner adds everyone else by typing an email and a password. You hand them that password yourself. GOST does not send mail.

## One board, one home

One copy of GOST is one household, or one client. It runs on a computer you control. Two families get two copies, and each copy keeps its own board.
